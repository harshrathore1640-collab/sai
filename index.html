Natural Stone & Architectural Surfaces
A Heda Group Company

Sai Granito India is rooted in Kishangarh, Rajasthan, one of India’s most recognised centres for marble and natural stone. The company’s journey began in 1982 with Shri Bal Mukund ji Heda and a vision built around material quality, long-term relationships and a lasting respect for natural stone.

Today, Sai Granito brings together careful stone selection, modern processing and a considered understanding of how marble, granite and distinctive imported surfaces shape architectural spaces. Every stone has its own colour, movement and character; our approach is to help each material find the right place in a project.

As part of the Heda Group, Sai Granito continues to build on its heritage while looking ahead to new ideas in design, finish and application.

A study in texture, tone and finish

Material Alchemists

A surface changes how a room feels. Polished, honed and textured finishes respond to light differently, while colour and veining can create calm, drama or an architectural statement. The right selection balances the aesthetic vision with the practical needs of the space.

Polished

Reflective surfaces reveal depth, contrast and the movement of natural veining.

Honed

A softer, more understated appearance that keeps attention on tone and texture.

Textured

Tactile character and surface variation add a distinctive architectural quality.

A closer look at surfaces

Texture, tone and finish in conversation.

Polished, honed and textured finishes change the way light moves across a surface. Ivory and beige tones can create a calm, open atmosphere; darker stone can introduce contrast and visual weight; expressive veining can become the focal point of a room. Always consider lighting, use, maintenance and the surrounding materials when selecting a finish.

A story rooted in Kishangarh.

Sai Granito India traces its beginnings to 1982, when Shri Bal Mukund ji Heda envisioned a company dedicated to excellence in stone. Based in Kishangarh, Rajasthan, and part of the Heda Group, the company continues to build on that foundation through material understanding, quality-focused work and long-term relationships. The legacy is not only about time passed, but about continuing to respect the natural character of stone while meeting the needs of contemporary spaces.
