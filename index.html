Our Heritage

Rooted in Kishangarh.

Sai Granito India is associated with the Heda Group and has a history dating back to 1982, when Shri Bal Mukund ji Heda began a journey shaped by a lasting appreciation for natural stone.

Based in Kishangarh, Rajasthan, the brand's story is connected with the region's long-standing marble and natural-stone trade.
